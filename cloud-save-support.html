Cloud Save+ Support

Cloud Save+ helps you back up, organize, download, and manage your files securely across your account.

Free plan

Every account includes 1 GB of free storage with manual and automatic backup. Sharing and receiving files are available on Cloud Save+ paid plans.

Backups and storage

  • Use Automatic Backup to keep supported content backed up.
  • Use manual upload whenever you want to save specific files.
  • If your account reaches its storage limit, you can delete existing files or upgrade your plan.

Subscriptions

Cloud Save+ offers paid storage plans. Purchases are managed through your Apple Account. You can restore an existing purchase from the Cloud Save+ subscription screen using Restore Purchases.

To change or cancel a subscription, use Manage Subscription in the app or manage subscriptions through your Apple Account settings. Cancelling stops future renewal; access remains available until the current paid period ends.

Need help?

If you need support with your account, backup, storage, or subscription, contact the Cloud Save+ team through the app's support channel.
