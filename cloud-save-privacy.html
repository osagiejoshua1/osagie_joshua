Cloud Save+ Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Cloud Save+ is a cloud storage and backup service. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how it is handled when you use the app.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you create an account or use Cloud Save+:

  • Account information: name, email address, user/account ID, and authentication information needed to sign you in and secure your account.
  • User content: files you choose to upload, back up, store, send, or receive, including photos, videos, audio files, documents, other file types, and direct messages or text content used in Cloud Save+ messaging features.
  • Subscription and purchase information: information about your Cloud Save+ plan, subscription status, product purchased, renewal or expiration status, and purchase history needed to provide the correct storage entitlement.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Cloud Save+ does not collect your payment card number, bank account number, credit score, salary, income, advertising data, or biometric body data. Payments for mobile subscriptions are processed by the applicable app-store billing provider, and Cloud Save+ does not receive your full payment-card details.

3. How We Use Information

We use collected information only as needed to provide and operate Cloud Save+ features, including to:

  • Create, authenticate, and manage user accounts.
  • Upload, store, back up, organize, download, share, receive, and delete user files.
  • Provide direct messaging and file-sharing functionality between users where available.
  • Determine and maintain the correct Free or paid storage entitlement for an account.
  • Restore purchases and maintain subscription access through the applicable paid-through period.
  • Protect accounts, prevent abuse, maintain service reliability, and provide customer support.

4. Advertising, Analytics, and Tracking

Cloud Save+ does not use collected personal data for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking. We do not use names, email addresses, uploaded files, messages, or purchase history to track users across other companies' apps or websites.

5. Cloud Storage and Service Providers

Cloud Save+ relies on hosting, database, cloud-storage, authentication, and app-store billing providers to operate the service. These providers may process information on our behalf only as necessary to deliver their services. App-store purchases are also subject to the privacy terms of the applicable platform provider.

6. User Files and Messages

Your uploaded files and messages are stored so Cloud Save+ can provide the features you request. They are not used for advertising. Access is limited to what is required to operate the service, comply with law, investigate abuse or security issues, or respond to a valid support request.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain account information and user content for as long as needed to provide Cloud Save+ and maintain the user's account, subject to legal and operational requirements. Users may delete individual stored files through the app. Where account-deletion controls are available, users may use them to request deletion of their account and associated data.

Ending or cancelling a paid subscription does not by itself delete stored files. Access and storage limits may change according to the account's current plan.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect account information and stored content. No internet or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so users should also protect their account credentials and devices.

9. Children's Privacy

Cloud Save+ is not designed specifically for children. Users should only use the service where permitted by applicable law and any age requirements that apply to their account or platform.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Cloud Save+ changes. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or support relating to Cloud Save+, use the support options available in the app or visit the Cloud Save+ Support page.
